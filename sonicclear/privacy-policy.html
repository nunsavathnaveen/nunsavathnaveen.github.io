SonicClear Privacy Policy

As of November 1, 2026

SonicClear is a speaker-cleaning and sound-diagnostic utility for Android. It has no user accounts, no sign-in, and does not sell or share personal data. The app works entirely offline for its core features (water ejection, dust removal, hearing test, speaker test, sound meter); an internet connection is used only to serve ads through Google AdMob.

SonicClear does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data. There is no account system and nothing is uploaded to our own servers, because we don't operate any.

Third-Party Services

SonicClear displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), approximate location, and app usage data to serve and measure ads, independently of SonicClear. This is governed by Google's own policies, not ours:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data in ads

SonicClear does not control what AdMob collects and does not receive a copy of it.

Consent (EEA, UK & Switzerland): if you are in one of these regions, SonicClear asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice anytime in the app under Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.

Data Retention & Children's Privacy

Since SonicClear stores nothing on any server, there is no personal data for us to retain, back up, or delete — microphone audio is processed in memory and discarded immediately after each reading.

SonicClear is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data through the app, contact us below and we will address it — though as noted, we do not operate a collection point for this data in the first place.

Your Choices

  • Personalized ads: opt out or reset your Android Advertising ID anytime in Settings → Privacy → Ads on your device.
  • Ad consent (EEA, UK & Switzerland): review or withdraw your consent anytime in SonicClear → Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Microphone access: revoke it anytime in Settings → Apps → SonicClear → Permissions; the Sound Meter feature simply won't work without it, everything else is unaffected.
  • Data deletion requests: not applicable — we don't store any personal data to delete. Contact us and we'll confirm this directly.

Changes to This Policy & Contact

We may update this policy as SonicClear's features change. Material changes will be reflected by a new "as of" date at the top of this page.
